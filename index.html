TECHNICAL EVENT 02 MIN 2 PER TEAM • 70 MINS

2. PROMPT BATTLE : GENERATIVE AI CHALLENGE

Prompt Battle is a team-based technical event where participants use AI tools to generate Images, Videos, and Working Models/Software/Apps based on given problem statements. It tests creativity, prompt-crafting skill, and the ability to translate ideas into AI-generated output — all within a fixed time limit.

Rules & Regulations:

  • Each team can have a minimum of two members.
  • The event conductor will provide the list of approved AI tools for each category.
  • If a team wishes to use any other AI tool, it must be requested and approved by the event conductor before use.
  • Internet usage is restricted to the approved AI tool only — no browsing other sites, forums, or search engines.
  • Problem statements and resources will be provided before the contest begins.
  • Participants must use only the specified AI tool(s) for their category to generate their output.
  • Do not copy-paste or reuse output from any external source, previous projects, or other participants — the output must be generated live during the event.
  • Do not discuss, share, or copy others' prompts, output, or approach during the contest. Any attempt will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • In case of any conflict, the decision of the jury panel will be final.

Event Structure (70 Minutes Total):

Phase Time What Happens
1. Choose Questions 5 min Theme/problem statement revealed for team's category (Image / Video / App); team selects/confirms their question.
2. Create & Submit Approach 15 min Team plans their approach/prompt strategy and submits it for record before execution.
3. Execute AI Tools 35 min Team generates output using approved AI tools, refines as needed, and finalizes submission.
4. Analysis & Results 15 min Judges evaluate output against the problem requirements; results announced.
TECHNICAL EVENT 03 INDIVIDUAL • OFFLINE LAB

3. CODE COMBAT : OFFLINE CODING CHALLENGE

Code Combat is an individual offline coding challenge inspired by platforms such as LeetCode and HackerRank. The competition is conducted in the college computer laboratory without Internet access and is designed to test participants' coding skills, logical thinking, algorithmic knowledge, and efficiency.

Participation: Strictly Individual Venue: College Computer Lab Only Internet: Strictly Prohibited Languages: C, Java, Python
STRICT ANTI-CHEATING RULE

Any participant found copying, reproducing, sharing, or using another participant's code or solution in any form will be IMMEDIATELY DISQUALIFIED WITHOUT WARNING. The participant will be removed from the competition, and NO APPEAL OR RECONSIDERATION will be permitted.

Rules & Regulations:

  • Participation is strictly individual.
  • Participants must use the computers provided in the college computer laboratory.
  • Internet access is strictly prohibited throughout the event.
  • Mobile phones, smart devices, and other external electronic devices are not permitted during the competition.
  • Participants must solve all problems using their own coding and problem-solving skills.
  • Copying code or solutions from another participant is strictly prohibited.
  • Sharing code, solutions, answers, or approaches with other participants is prohibited.
  • Pre-written programs or solutions prepared before the event cannot be submitted.
  • Participants cannot access online coding platforms, search engines, websites, or online references during the event.
  • Any attempt to bypass the no-internet restriction will result in immediate disqualification.
  • The decision of the judges and organizing committee will be final and binding.

Programming Languages Allowed:

1C
2Java
3Python

Participants may choose the programming language they are most comfortable with. Submitted solutions are evaluated with sample and hidden test cases.

NON-TECHNICAL EVENT 02 CREATIVE PICTIONARY

2. SKETCH HUNT : VISUAL COMMUNICATION

How The Game Works:

STEP 1

Teams register before the event begins.

STEP 2

One participant from the team receives a secret clue from the event coordinator.

STEP 3

The participant communicates the clue exclusively through drawings.

STEP 4

Remaining team members guess within the time limit. Correct answers earn points.

14 General Rules:

1. No Words — Must not write words, letters, numbers, initials, abbreviations, or spelling clues.
2. No Speaking — The sketcher must remain completely silent (no talking, sounds, or mouthing words).
3. No Actions — Acting, hand gestures, body movements, or sign language are strictly prohibited.
4. Drawing Only — The clue must be communicated strictly through the sketch.
5. No Direct Indication — Pointing at objects, people, posters, or screens is not allowed.
6. No Mobile Phones — Phones, internet searches, AI tools, or electronic devices cannot be used.
7. No Outside Help — Participants cannot receive clues or assistance from spectators or organizers.
8. No Copying — Must not copy another team's sketch or observe their clues.
9. No Clue Sharing — A participant must not reveal or show the secret clue to other teams.
10. Time Limit — Every clue must be guessed within the specified time limit.
11. Team Communication — Guessers may discuss among themselves; the drawer must remain silent.
12. No Disturbance — Teams must not intentionally distract or interfere with others.
13. Fair Play — Any attempt to cheat or bypass rules results in penalty or disqualification.
14. Organizer's Decision — Decision of coordinators and judges will be final in all disputes.
Tie-Breaker Protocol

If two or more teams have the same final score:

  • A new secret clue will be provided.
  • The same drawing-only rules will apply.
  • The team that guesses correctly first will win the tie-breaker.
  • Additional clues will be given if needed until a winner is determined.
Disqualification Criteria
  • Writing the answer or spelling clues.
  • Speaking, gesturing, or acting clues.
  • Using phones, internet, or taking spectator help.
  • Copying sketches or intentionally disturbing others.
Core Philosophy

“The event is based on pure visual communication through sketching. The person receiving the clue must communicate only through drawing.”
